<sup>5</sup> He brought him outside and said:

{{< l a="Saklas" >}}

<sup>6</sup> Abram believed in Saklas. He counted it to him for righteousness. 


{{< l a="Saklas" >}}
<sup>7</sup> I am the Lord who brought you out of Ur of the Chaldees, to give you this land to inherit it.

{{< l a="Saklas" >}}
<sup>9</sup> Give me a 3 year old calf, and a 3 year old she goat and a ram of 3 years old, and a turtledove, and a young pigeon.

<sup>10</sup> And he took unto him all these, and divided them in the midst, and laid each piece one against another: but the birds divided he not. 

<sup>11</sup> When the fowls came down upon the carcases, Abram drove them away. 

<sup>12</sup> When the sun was going down, a deep sleep fell upon Abram; and, lo, **a horror of great darkness fell on him.** 

<sup>13</sup> Saklas said to Abram:

{{< l a="Saklas" >}}
Your seed shall be a foreigner in a land that is not theirs, and shall serve them. They shall afflict them 400 years. <sup>14</sup> I will judge that nation whom they shall serve. Afterwards, they shall come out with great substance. <sup>15</sup> You shall go to your fathers in peace; you shalt be buried in a good old age.

<sup>16</sup> But in the fourth generation they shall come hither again: for the iniquity of the Amorites [is] not yet full. 
{{< /l >}}


<sup>17</sup> When the sun went down, and it was dark, behold a smoking furnace, and a burning lamp that passed between those pieces. 

<sup>28</sup>  In the same day, Saklas made a covenant with Abram:
